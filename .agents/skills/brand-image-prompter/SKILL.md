# Brand-Locked Image Prompt System

Never write image prompts off the top of your head. This skill wraps any visual idea in your signature aesthetic DNA so every generated image looks like part of one unified brand world.

## Brand Visual Architecture

* **Camera & Optics**: 35mm / 50mm prime lens, f/1.8 aperture, shallow depth of field, natural bokeh, sharp tactile foreground focus.
* **Lighting & Atmosphere**: Cinematic editorial lighting, soft directional side-lighting, subtle atmospheric volumetrics, clean negative space.
* **Color Grading**: Film emulation (Kodak Portra / Cinestill tone curve), deep obsidian shadows, desaturated realistic skin tones, high dynamic range.
* **Composition**: Dynamic editorial framing, rule of thirds, clean minimal backgrounds without cluttered CGI artifacts.

---

## Universal Negative Prompt (AI Slop Filter)

Every prompt automatically excludes:
`cartoon, oversaturated, 3d render, plastic skin, distorted hands, generic stock photo, corporate clip art, neon halos, oversmoothed textures, lowres, watermark, text artifacts`

---

## Prompt Formula

```text
[Subject Action / Focal Concept], [Art Direction & Medium], shot on [Lens & Camera specs], [Lighting & Color Temperature], [Color Palette & Grading], clean minimalist composition, 8k resolution, editorial commercial quality --ar 16:9 --style raw --v 6.1
```
